This site is my home for writing in public.

I want to share the practical side of learning open source: the commands that worked, the mistakes that taught me something, the tools I keep coming back to, and the small projects that make me a better engineer.

## What I Will Publish

- Notes from Linux, Docker, Python, AWS, Azure, and GitHub experiments.
- Short tutorials that solve one clear problem.
- Project updates from repositories I am building.
- Lessons from bugs, deployments, and open-source collaboration.

The goal is simple: make my work easier to discover and make each post useful to someone else.

## How I Will Keep It Going

I do not need every post to be huge. A useful post can be a five-minute note with a command, a fix, and a clear explanation. Consistency matters more than perfection.

If you find something helpful here, connect with me on GitHub or LinkedIn.
